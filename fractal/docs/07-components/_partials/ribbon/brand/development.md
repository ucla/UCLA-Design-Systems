Building a brand ribbon is simple:

- `.ucla-ribbon.ucla-ribbon--brand` - Container and variant
  - `.ucla-ribbon__text` - Text _(Can be any heading/element)_

<div class="ucla-ribbon ucla-ribbon--brand">
    <h2 class="ucla-ribbon__text">Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetuer adipiscing</h2>
</div>
