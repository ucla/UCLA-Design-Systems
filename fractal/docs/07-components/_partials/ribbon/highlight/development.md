Building a highlight ribbon is simple:

- `.ucla-ribbon.ucla-ribbon--highlight` - Container and variant
  - `.ucla-ribbon__text` - Text _(Can be any heading/element)_

<div class="ucla-ribbon ucla-ribbon--highlight">
    <h3 class="ucla-ribbon__text">85% of undergraduates enroll in the college.</h3>
</div>
