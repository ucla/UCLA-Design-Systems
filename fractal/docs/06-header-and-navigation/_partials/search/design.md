Search allows visitors to quickly find the information they’re looking for.

#### When to use

Use once the scale and type of content on your site is conducive to searching. 

#### Anatomy

<img alt="Search Anatomy" class="ucla-break-container" src="/theme-assets/img/docs/navigation/search-anatomy.svg" />

**1. Close Button**
  - Search Icon toggles to close button, clicking again with close entire overlay

**2. Overlay**
  - White background overlay extends below navigation and covers top of page content

**3. Text Field**

**4. Search Entry Icon**

**5. Search Text**
  - Appears as it's entered by user

**6. Clear Text Button**
  - Clears any search text entered in the field

#### Tablet &amp; Mobile

![Search Mobile Anatomy](/theme-assets/img/docs/navigation/search-mobile.svg)

**Mobile Search Field**
  - On mobile, Search is everpresent but tapping on field activates onscreen keyboard or input device and displays the clear text button.

#### Best Practices

Note that this feature is only as good as the search engine its connected to. Poor or irrelevant search results are a liability that can lead people to exit your site and look elsewhere.
